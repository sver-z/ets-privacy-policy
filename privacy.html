ETS - Expense Tracking Simplified

Privacy Policy | Effective date: 7 October 2026

SverZ Apps ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ETS - Expense Tracking Simplified ("ETS" or "the app") handles information.

1. Information Stored by ETS

ETS is designed as an offline-first expense tracker. Expense information you enter, including amount, date, category, payment mode, merchant and notes, is stored locally on your device.

Your budget setting and currency preference are also stored locally on your device.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

ETS does not have user accounts, does not require you to provide your name or email address, and does not intentionally transmit your expense records, budget information or currency preferences to SverZ Apps or third parties.

ETS does not sell or share your expense data for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Internet and Third-Party Services

The current ETS app does not provide a cloud account, online backup service, analytics feature or advertising service. The app may contain standard Android and Capacitor components required to package and run it. Any future third-party service or data transmission will be reviewed, and this policy will be updated before the relevant feature is introduced.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Expense information is stored locally on your device. You can delete individual expenses through the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data may remove locally stored ETS data, subject to Android and device behaviour.

SverZ Apps does not maintain a remote copy of your expense records that we can delete on your behalf.

5. Security

ETS is designed to keep expense information on your device rather than transmit it to a remote server. You are responsible for securing your device and any device backup or export mechanism you choose to use.

6. Children's Privacy

ETS is a general-purpose expense tracking utility and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the way ETS handles data changes, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the new practices. The effective date will also be updated.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns about ETS, please use the developer contact information provided on the ETS Google Play Store listing for SverZ Apps.
